How Gmail access is used

Sign in to Betlysync first. Connecting Gmail is optional and separate from signing in: Betlysync requests Gmail permissions only after you choose Connect Gmail and Google asks you to approve access.

  • Read access: Used to identify and process supported short-term-rental emails, including reservation, guest message, listing, and available payout information.
  • Send access: Betlysync is adding replies to supported guest conversations. When available, sending is used only when you intentionally send or reply to a guest message through Betlysync.

Betlysync does not send arbitrary email automatically on your behalf, send unrelated marketing email through your Gmail account, or currently perform automatic AI sending.
