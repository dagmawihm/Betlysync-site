Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Betlysync ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our short-term-rental host application. The operator and contact details are listed in section 12. Betlysync organizes hosting information from supported platform emails, currently including Airbnb.

This public information site has no JavaScript, analytics, cookies, or tracking pixels added by Betlysync. The application data practices described below concern the Betlysync app; the website hosting provider may process basic request information to serve these pages.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and profile information: Information supplied through authentication, such as your name, email address, and account identifier, where provided.
  • Connected Gmail information: Connected account details, authorization credentials needed to maintain the connection, and email content and metadata accessed under the permissions you approve.
  • Hosting information: Reservation details, guest messages, stay dates, property or listing information, and payout information extracted from supported short-term-rental emails. Guest profile images may also be present in those emails.
  • App-generated data: Conversation records, synchronization records, and outgoing host messages when sending is available and used.
  • Technical and service information: Basic operational information, such as request, error, and security logs, necessary to operate, troubleshoot, and secure the app.

3. Google and Gmail data

Signing in to Betlysync does not by itself authorize Gmail access. Connecting Gmail is optional. After you choose Connect Gmail, Google asks you to approve permissions. Betlysync may then access Gmail under the permissions you approve.

Read: Gmail read-only

The intended read permission is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. Betlysync uses read access to locate and process supported short-term-rental emails and extract relevant hosting information. This permission grants mailbox read access; the product uses that access for the supported hosting functionality described here.

Send: Gmail send

The intended send permission is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. Betlysync is adding the ability to reply to supported guest conversations through connected Gmail accounts. When available, this permission is used only when you intentionally choose to send a supported guest reply from Betlysync.

Betlysync does not use Gmail send access to send arbitrary marketing or unrelated email. It does not automatically send arbitrary email on your behalf and does not currently implement automatic AI sending.

4. How information is used

Betlysync uses information to:

  • Synchronize reservations and provide stay and property context.
  • Display guest conversations and maintain conversation history.
  • Calculate and display payout information derived from available source data.
  • Send host-authorized guest replies when that functionality is available.
  • Maintain the Gmail connection and operate, troubleshoot, and secure the service.

Google Maps Platform and Places address search

Betlysync integrates Google Maps Platform, including Places API (New), for optional property-address search. When this feature is enabled and you use it, the address search text you type is sent through Betlysync's backend to Google Places, which returns suggested addresses. Selecting a suggestion requests place details, including address components and any provider attributions.

Selected address information populates the property form. You can review and correct it, add an apartment or unit, and save it as part of your property information. You can also enter an address manually instead of selecting a Google suggestion. Choosing manual entry does not undo search requests already sent.

Google processes information received through this feature under the Google Privacy Policy, which is incorporated by reference for Google's processing. Address search is separate from connecting Gmail and does not require Gmail access. Availability may be limited while the feature is disabled or being tested.

5. Storage and retention

Necessary application data may be stored in Betlysync's backend infrastructure, including extracted hosting information, conversation records, and data needed to maintain your connection. Qualifying guest profile images found in supported platform emails may be cached privately for app functionality.

Retention depends on the data and the operational purpose for which it is held; no fixed retention period is specified here. Disconnecting Gmail or revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not by itself mean previously stored application data is deleted. Contact us about stored data or deletion requests.

6. Sharing

We do not sell data. Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers as necessary to operate Betlysync. When you intentionally send a guest reply, the message and necessary routing information are shared through Gmail with the intended recipient and any relevant platform messaging service.

Connected Google account data is not provided to advertisers for advertising purposes. Processing and sharing of Google API data are subject to the policy commitments in section 7.

7. Google API data and Limited Use

Betlysync's use of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Google API data, including information derived from it, is used for the user-facing functionality described in this policy. Its use, transfer, and any human access must stay within that policy's permitted purposes and conditions. It is not used for advertising.

8. Your controls

You may disconnect Gmail from Betlysync, revoke Betlysync's Google authorization through your Google Account, or stop using the service. Features that depend on Gmail access will no longer synchronize or send through that connection after access is removed. These controls do not delete emails already in Gmail or messages already sent.

For questions or requests concerning your information, use the contact details below.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

Betlysync is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to Betlysync, contact us so we can address it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. If Google data practices change, we will provide any notices and obtain any additional consent required before using data in a new way.
